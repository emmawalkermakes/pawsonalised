Pawsonalised is an independent hobby blog about personalised things for pets and pet people in Australia: ID tags, collars, Christmas gifts, keepsakes and the occasional bit of household chaos that comes with sharing your home with animals.

It's written in Brisbane, where pets deal with long hot summers, storm season and the odd possum on the fence. That shapes a lot of the advice here: which materials survive the heat, what to put on a tag so a frightened dog gets home quickly, and how to choose gifts that are safe as well as cute.

## How ideas are chosen

- **Safety first.** If a product idea could be a choking, chewing or heat hazard, I'll say so.
- **Local rules, linked.** Where registration or microchipping rules matter, posts link to the council or state government page rather than paraphrasing from memory.
- **No fake reviews or invented statistics.**
- **Not veterinary advice.** For anything about your pet's health, talk to your vet.

## Disclosure

This is a hobby blog. I enjoy making things, and now and then I may feature something I make myself. When I do, I'll say so clearly, right next to the item. There are no sponsored posts.
